I am a final-year PhD candidate in Computer Science at the University of Exeter, advised by Chunbo Luo. I work on vision-language models and open-vocabulary detection for remote sensing and disaster monitoring. My research studies how detectors behave under distribution shift, when the test setting differs from training in viewpoint, vocabulary, or hazard type, and designs methods that keep vision-language detectors reliable across these gaps. Recent projects cover cross-view geometry and cross-vocabulary generalization, including mechanistic analysis of where fine-tuned detectors fail.
